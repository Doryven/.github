# Doryven

**Engineering technology for networks that move things.**
Simulation, optimisation and automation for rail, maritime and multimodal freight.

[doryven.com](https://doryven.com)

---

## What we work on

**Simulation and digital twins** — network models that reproduce how a system actually
behaves under load, not how it is supposed to behave on paper.

**Optimisation** — scheduling, routing and energy problems where the constraints are real
and the search space is large.

**Automation** — turning a modelling result into something that runs on its own, on a
schedule, with an audit trail.

## Platforms

Open-source simulators for freight networks, each modelling a different mode:

| | |
|---|---|
| **NeTrainSim** | Rail freight networks — energy, motion and train dynamics |
| **ShipNetSim** | Maritime networks — ship energy and route performance |
| **CargoNetSim** | Multimodal freight — rail, sea and road as one network |

Released under GPL-3.0 and maintained by Doryven. *Repositories are being migrated into
this organisation; until that completes they remain at their original locations.*

## Products

**Doryven Register** — attendance, office hours and class activities for higher education.
Works standalone or installed into an LMS over LTI 1.3.
