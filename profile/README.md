### Engineering technology for networks that move things

Simulation, optimisation and automation for rail, maritime and multimodal freight.

**[doryven.com](https://doryven.com)**

---

### What we work on

**Simulation and digital twins** — network models that reproduce how a system behaves under
load, not how it is supposed to behave on paper.

**Optimisation** — scheduling, routing and energy problems where the constraints are real and
the search space is large.

**Automation** — turning a modelling result into something that runs on its own, on a
schedule, with an audit trail.

### Platforms

Open-source simulators for freight networks, each modelling a different mode.

| Platform | Domain |
|---|---|
| **NeTrainSim** | Rail freight — energy, motion and train dynamics |
| **ShipNetSim** | Maritime — ship energy and route performance |
| **CargoNetSim** | Multimodal freight — rail, sea and road as one network |

Released under GPL-3.0 and maintained by Doryven.
*Repositories are being migrated into this organisation.*

### Products

**Doryven Register** — attendance, office hours and class activities for higher education.
Runs standalone or installs into a learning management system over LTI 1.3.
