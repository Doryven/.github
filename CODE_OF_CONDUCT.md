# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and be civil. Critique work, not people. Assume the person you are
replying to is competent and acting in good faith, and say so plainly when you disagree.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Scope

Applies in every Doryven repository, issue, pull request and discussion, and to anyone
representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **conduct@doryven.com**. Reports are read by the project owner and
kept confidential. Include what happened, where, and links if the exchange is public.

We will respond, decide, and say what we decided. Depending on severity the outcome may
be a warning, removal of content, or a ban. Maintainers who do not uphold this standard
are subject to the same consequences as anyone else.
